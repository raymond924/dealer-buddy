    Claims, step by step

    Report the event

    Report every claim to us on 021 300 2154 within 5 working days of the incident or breakdown.

    Get a quote

    Take the customer's vehicle for a quote: to a manufacturer-appointed franchised dealer if it's still under the original manufacturer warranty, otherwise to an RMI-registered facility. Not sure where? Our claims department will guide you.

    Submit the quote

    The repairing dealer sends the quote to our claims department at claims@globalmotor.co.za.

    Claim validation

    Our claims department reviews the quote. If the claim is approved, we issue an authorisation number and the authorised amount.

    Authorisation before repairs

    Repairs can't begin until the claims department has given authorisation.

    No work before authorisation

    Claims won't be considered if repairs started before authorisation was received.

    Valid invoices only

    We pay invoices that meet the SARS definition of a valid tax invoice.

    The authorised amount

    We pay the authorised amount. If the invoice is higher, the shortfall is the customer's responsibility.

    Claim expiry

    If repairs on an authorised claim don't start within 90 days of authorisation, the claim is closed as "not taken up".

    Got questions?

    Who is DealerBuddy?

    DealerBuddy is part of the BuddyCentral Group, with over 25 years in the insurance industry. We build risk solutions for the dealership and fleet industries: mechanical warranties, tyre and rim cover, service and maintenance plans, exterior and interior maintenance, and fleet assistance. DealerBuddy is an authorised financial services provider, FSP 46862.

    Who can sell DealerBuddy products?

    Dealerships that register as a DealerBuddy selling dealer. Fleet and rent-to-own businesses can partner with us directly for Fleet Assistance.

    How do I become a selling dealer?

    Complete the dealer registration form and send it with your supporting documents (company registration, VAT certificate, bank confirmation, director's ID, FSP or representative details and more). The full checklist is on our Partner with us page.

    How do I report a claim?

    Call our claims line on 021 300 2154 within 5 working days of the incident or breakdown. Then take the vehicle for a quote and have the repairer send it to claims@globalmotor.co.za.

    Where should the vehicle be quoted and repaired?

    If the vehicle is still under the original manufacturer warranty, at a manufacturer-appointed franchised dealer. In all other cases, at an RMI (Retail Motor Industry) registered facility.

    Can repairs start before the claim is authorised?

    No. Repairs can only begin once our claims department has issued an authorisation number. Claims where work started before authorisation won't be considered.

    What if the repair costs more than the authorised amount?

    We pay the authorised amount. Any shortfall on the invoice is the customer's responsibility.

    How large is the service provider network?

    Over 1 200 trusted service providers nationwide, for mechanical work, maintenance and emergency assistance.
