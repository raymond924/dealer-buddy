Got questions?

Who is DealerBuddy?

DealerBuddy is part of the BuddyCentral Group, a trusted leader in the insurance industry with over 25 years of expertise. Our revolutionary, innovative products offer comprehensive risk solutions designed for the fleet and dealership industries. DealerBuddy – FSP 46862.

Who is DealerBuddy for?

Dealerships, fleet owners and rent-to-own businesses. Our goal is to streamline vehicle management, so you can focus on your customers while we handle the complexities behind the scenes.

How do I become an authorised dealer?

Complete our dealer sign-up: your dealership and banking details, compliance information, branch and contact details, supporting documents and the products to be loaded. The full list is on our Partner with us page.

How do I report a claim?

Report all claims to us on 021 300 2154 within 5 working days of the incident or breakdown. Then take the vehicle for a quote, and the repairing dealer submits the quote to claims@globalmotor.co.za.
