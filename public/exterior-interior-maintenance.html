Got questions?

How do I report a claim?

Report all claims to us on 021 300 2154 within 5 working days of the incident or breakdown. Then take the vehicle for a quote, and the repairing dealer submits the quote to claims@globalmotor.co.za.

Where should the vehicle be quoted and repaired?

If the vehicle is still under the Original Manufacturer Warranty, at a manufacturer-appointed franchised dealer. In all other cases, at an RMI (Retail Motor Industry) registered facility. Unsure? Our claims department can guide you.

Can repairs start before the claim is authorised?

No. Repairs cannot begin until the claims department has provided authorisation. Claims will not be considered if repairs have already started before authorisation was received.
